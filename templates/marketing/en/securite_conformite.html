Security & compliance

Confidentiality by design

Our security measures aren't marketing promises — here's concretely how the platform protects your data.

Two-factor authentication (MFA)

Every expert and admin account must enable TOTP-based authentication (Google Authenticator, Authy or equivalent) in addition to a password before accessing any file.

Strict per-file access control

A client only sees their own files. An expert only sees files assigned to them or opened to the team. Every access is checked on every request, never assumed.

Time-stamped, hashed documents

Every document uploaded or signed receives a SHA-256 cryptographic fingerprint at the time of upload. Any later modification of the file would break that fingerprint — a mechanism used to certify the integrity of electronically signed documents.

Activity log

Sensitive actions (sign-in, document upload, status change, file creation, payment, signature) are logged with a timestamp, to ensure traceability when needed.

Protected passwords

Passwords are never stored in plain text: they are salted and hashed (industry-standard algorithm via Werkzeug/PBKDF2) before being saved to the database.

Traceable e-signature

Every signature records the signer's full legal name, the signed document's fingerprint, the IP address and the user agent at the time of signing, with a certificate available for later review.

Good to know

What this page doesn't claim

We prefer transparency over overselling: the platform is not currently certified SOC 2, ISO 27001 or equivalent, and has not yet undergone an independent external security audit. These measures describe the technical architecture actually implemented today, not a label obtained. If your organization has specific compliance requirements (financial sector, regulated institution), contact us to discuss them concretely before opening a file.
