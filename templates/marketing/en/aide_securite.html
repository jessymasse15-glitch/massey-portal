Secure my account

Simple, concrete steps to protect access to your account and files. To see how the platform protects your data on its side, see our Security & compliance page instead.

Turn on two-factor authentication

Even though it's only mandatory for our staff, we recommend every client enable two-factor authentication (a code generated by an app like Google Authenticator or Authy). It blocks access to your account even if your password were discovered.

{% if current_user %} Turn on two-factor authentication {% else %} Log in to enable it {% endif %}

Choose a strong, unique password

Use a long password (12 characters or more), different from your other accounts, ideally generated and stored by a password manager. Never share it, even with someone claiming to be a member of our team.

Watch out for phishing

We will never ask for your password or two-factor code by email or phone. Before clicking a link in an email, check that it leads to our official domain. If in doubt, contact us directly rather than replying to the message.

Compromised account: act fast

If you think your account has been accessed without your authorization, change your password immediately from your profile, then report it to us via the assistance request below so we can review account activity and help you.

Need help with your account?

Lost access, email change, billing question: our team can help you directly.
