Privacy policy

How we handle your data

Data collected

Depending on your use of the site, we may collect: your name and contact details (email, phone) when creating an account, booking a call, or sending a message via the Contact page; information related to your file (description of your need, uploaded documents, exchanges); technical data strictly necessary for the site to function (login session, security activity log).

Purposes of processing

This data is used to: assess and process your service request; track your file in the secure client area; contact you in connection with a file or request; ensure the security of the platform (authentication, anomaly detection, logging).

Legal basis and professional confidentiality

Processing your data is part of the performance or preparation of a professional engagement, and is subject to the confidentiality rules applicable to the practice of law and the protection of personal information.

Data sharing

Your data is not sold. It may be shared with technical providers strictly necessary for the service to function (cloud hosting, online payment processing), to the extent required to perform those services, and with any authority where required by law.

Retention

Data related to a file is retained for as long as necessary for the engagement, then for the retention period applicable to professional files. Messages sent via the Contact page without follow-up may be deleted periodically.

Security

The technical measures in place to protect your data are detailed on our Security & compliance page (two-factor authentication, password and document hashing, per-file access control, logging).

Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or, subject to legal and professional retention obligations, deletion of your personal data by contacting us via our Contact page.

Cookies

The site only uses cookies strictly necessary for it to function (maintaining your login session). No advertising or third-party tracking cookies are used at this time.
