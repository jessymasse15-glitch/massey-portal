FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How does an engagement start?

A professional engagement arises only from an accepted engagement letter. Opening a file through our form or booking a call does not, by itself, create an engagement — it's a scoping step that lets us assess your need, check for conflicts of interest, and propose a suitable pack.

What are your response times?

We aim for a fast first response to any initial request. File processing times vary with complexity and are specified in the engagement letter.

Is my information and my documents confidential?

Yes. Every file is siloed: only the client concerned and the team members assigned to it can access it. Our technical security measures (two-factor authentication, document hashing, access logging) are detailed on our Security & compliance page.

Do you only work on the Haitian market?

Our base is the Haitian market, but a significant part of our practice involves cross-border deals with foreign partners — hence our Massey CrossBorder pack and our bilingual French/English platform.

How is pricing set?

Each pack is calibrated to the actual complexity of the file rather than a generic flat rate. The final amount is always confirmed in writing in the engagement letter before work begins — see our Pricing page.

What is Massey AI?

Massey AI is the name of our technology platform, organized into 5 pillars (Legal, Contract, Transaction, Tax and Regulatory & Compliance Intelligence). Some features already rely on real tools today (clause library, tax tracking, due diligence checklists, document search); generative AI capabilities are being rolled out.

Can I track my file online?

Yes. Every client has a secure client area to track their file's progress, exchange messages, upload and sign documents, and track their tax and compliance obligations.
