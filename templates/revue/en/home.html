At the crossroads of Haitian law, taxation, international trade, and legal technology, Massey Law Review offers a space for reflection and exchange for practitioners, researchers, and decision-makers.

Our conviction is simple: doctrinal rigor should never be foreign to business practice. Every piece we publish seeks to shed light on a concrete question — a clause, a structure, an implementation difficulty — in light of positive law and transactional practice.

Editorial mission

Massey Law Review publishes analyses, case studies, and practice notes on contract law, business taxation, and cross-border transactions, with particular attention to the Haitian market and its trade zones.

The review is aimed at lawyers, tax specialists, entrepreneurs, and investors who wish to understand not only the rule of law, but how it applies concretely to the conduct of business.

Editorial board

The editorial line is carried by the Massey Contracts & Tax team, supported by external contributions from practitioners and invited researchers who submit pieces according to the guidelines described on the Submissions page.

Every piece undergoes careful editorial review before publication, covering both the rigor of the analysis and the clarity of the presentation.

Independence and rigor

The review maintains complete editorial independence from matters otherwise handled by the firm. Opinions expressed in published articles are those of their authors and do not constitute legal advice applicable to any particular situation.

Readers seeking analysis applied to their own matter are invited to open a file with Massey Contracts & Tax rather than rely on the texts published here.

Contact us

For any editorial question, submission proposal, or forum-related request, use the form on the Submissions page or write to the review's editorial team.
