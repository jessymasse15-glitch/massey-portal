Author guidelines

Submitted texts must address contract law, business taxation, international trade law, or legal technology, with an explicit link to practice.

Expected format: Word or PDF document, a 150-to-250-word abstract, footnotes for references. Suggested length: 3,000 to 8,000 words for an article, 800 to 1,500 words for a practice note.

Every submission is acknowledged, then reviewed by the editorial board. Possible statuses are: received, under review, revisions requested, accepted, rejected, or published. The author is notified by email at every status change.
